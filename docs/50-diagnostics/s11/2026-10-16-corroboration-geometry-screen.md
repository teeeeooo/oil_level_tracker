## Source-level explanation and decision

`phase_candidate_assembler.py` passes diagnostic path sinks to the material
and raster-material generators and retains their actual sector samples by
candidate identity. The ordinary/calibrated peer rows in this population have
no such captured path. `phase_frame_detection.py` forwards that map to
`measure_oil_interfaces`; its `_measure_native_path` reports absence rather
than inventing a horizontal path. `_cross_representation_support` instead
uses eligible different-family candidates, scalar Y proximity and material,
coverage and artifact terms. It does not consume native-contour agreement.

This is a representation/availability boundary, not a newly demonstrated
serializer defect. It also does not prove that every future source lacks a
usable contour. The [design owner](../../20-architecture/s11-physical-interface-evidence-repair-design.md#independent-corroboration)
now distinguishes captured generator paths, explicitly re-observed raster
contour hypotheses and scalar-center measurements before a successor consumer.

**Do not add a both-native hard gate to the current support function.** It
would leave no positive contribution available in this population, including
the accepted material pair. This is a support-stage consequence, not a claim
that a completed detector lost 11 final observations. Nor does the absence
justify bypassing independence or replacing a missing contour with a median Y.
A successor still needs actual current-raster localization, independent
region/edge lineage and physical-role controls before authority. No such
classifier or replacement gate was selected or promoted in this experiment.
