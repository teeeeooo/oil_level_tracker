## Preregistered hypotheses and consumers

Run these finite arms once, with unchanged default sequence settings and an
unmodified baseline. All candidates, coordinates and raw scores remain present.

| Arm | Intervention | Question |
|---|---|---|
| baseline | Original current resolver; require complete saved-detection equality | Is the saved full-window causal input reproducible? |
| negative_anchor_demotion | At the authority return, only reviewed opposed ANCHOR becomes CONTINUATION | Is removal of independent anchor privilege sufficient to remove harmful output? |

These are diagnostic interventions, not rules to ship. Retain each original
reason, tier, material conflict and phase identity alongside its explicitly

acceptance is preservation evidence, not an anchor-generating input. Negative
subtype uncertainty stays intact: opposed Oil does not necessarily mean a
physically certified wall or non-interface of every material.

The wrapper binds every authority call to the complete raw input order and
checks all candidate fields except the two original owner-added context keys.
It changes no assignment sorting, confirmation windows, source preferences,
phase/selector thresholds, initial-state setting or Foam policy. Runtime patches
must be restored and source/input hashes rechecked after execution.

## Output-level validation and decision rule

Execute the real Oil owner, independent Foam composition, tracking-sample
projection, domain events, CSV exporter, report presentation and graph renderer.
The diagnostic result container is REVIEW_REQUIRED; automatic Recipe judgment,
full HTML/source-context generation, GUI and independent human report reading
are not claimed. Clearly mark comparison graphs as oracle diagnostics.

Join every selected source/index/Y back to the original same-frame raw candidate;
verify CSV values independently. Record all changed selections, allowed-owner
sets, material phase, candidate member states, Oil missing/numeric sampled runs,
Foam runs/validity and report extrema. A missing sample is not interpolation;
run spans end at the last sampled timestamp and are not precise physical durations.

Read success at context scale, not as a demand for all candidates: record whether
an accepted alternative is selected, whether a previously useful selection is
lost, and whether known wrong extrema persist. New unreviewed winners are not
improvements merely because their Y or confidence looks plausible. Sparse labels
cannot determine the whole duration of incorrect ownership. Any positive effect
of an oracle is a consumer feasibility result, never automatic detector efficacy.

Retain both adverse and useful effects of every arm. Do not tune another threshold
or expand the reviewed set after seeing results. Further distinct investigation
requires a separately recorded question; none of these arms permits a production
witness or a new Windows comparison by itself. Canonical Windows nine-segment,
FULL endpoint, independent Foam and actual report-only acceptance remain open.

not proof that new extrema describe the physical movement. All five arms retain
one report-level Foam episode. In positive/combined arms, the newly selected
39.5 s Oil Y838 triggers the existing final-Oil alias rejection of Foam Y841,
shifting the sampled first Foam observation from 39.5 to 40 s. This half-second
change alone is not a context failure. Negative-only veto instead preserves
40 s Foam Y840 but selects Oil Y831, causing topology flags and false per-series
validity. Raw Foam count alone would miss that adverse composition effect.

The post-run all-event audit joins all 143 retained domain events to their CSV
representative frame, timestamp and reported Oil/Foam levels. In addition to
extrema changes, positive-authority-only creates OIL_DROP_START at 41.5 s while
the combined arm does not. This new motion event is not independently qualified;
its appearance is not called either a proven recovery or a proven false event.
Verifier v2 adds this full-event check; the original v1 receipt stays immutable.

**Disposition: partial consumer feasibility, no automatic detector candidate.**
The combined intervention demonstrates useful selected-point recovery without

repair must supply both qualified positive and negative member evidence and
carry it through physical history/ownership and independent Foam composition.
