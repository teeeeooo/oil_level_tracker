# S11-R11 Detector Architecture Reset

## Purpose

R11 replaces accumulated version-coupled detector ownership with explicit raw
evidence, candidate authority, sequence path and material-composition owners.
The reset reuses proven algorithms; it is not a from-scratch detector rewrite.
Behavior-preserving structural commits precede intentional R11 result changes.

## One final observation owner

Current-frame processing produces immutable evidence and same-frame candidate
proposals. It does not own public Oil/state. The completed-window sequence
resolver remains the only owner of numeric Oil and image-supported state. Foam
episode publication remains independent, while a preliminary material-track
view may provide candidate identity opposition without publishing Foam or Oil.

No current-frame selected row, compatibility tracker value, initial state,
public Foam state or retrospective projection may become fallback numeric Oil.

## Typed evidence boundary

Control flow consumes semantic evidence views rather than direct versioned
dictionary keys. At minimum each Oil proposal exposes typed boundary, material,
motion, optics, artifact, texture, topology and provenance evidence. Existing
dictionary fields remain only at compatibility/debug projection boundaries
during migration.

Authority evaluation returns an explicit decision containing tier, one primary
reason and failed gates. Anchor routes are individually testable policies; a
candidate cannot silently bypass a failed route through an unreported Boolean
alternative.

## Current-frame composition

Frame preparation, raster/motion extraction, candidate generation, Artifact
filtering and debug projection are separate responsibilities. Candidate
families may use different generators but enter sequence evaluation through the
same typed evidence contract and bounded admission policy.

User Artifact templates remain explicit negative geometry. A match at or above
the accepted floor is hard-invalid; zero match is not opposition. Frame-level
“some Artifact rejected” provenance is distinct from selected-candidate match.

## Sequence decomposition

Completed-window Oil resolution is composed from bounded owners:

- candidate admission and reserved representation;
- candidate authority;
- recurring/static-track opposition;
- calibrated motion bootstrap;
- cluster and trajectory construction;
- global observation path;
- run/spike/reacquisition bounds; and
- final same-frame observation projection.

Each stage consumes and returns immutable typed rows. Only final projection may
set selected bits, public Oil/state or sequence flags.

## Calibrated bootstrap contract

Calibration bootstrap is no-observation recovery, not whole-window authority.
Motion keyframes must be locally distributed. A seed segment is bounded by its
first/last qualifying keyframe plus a small explicit edge and is split when the
keyframe gap exceeds the configured limit. Keyframes may promote only their
bounded segment; future evidence cannot retroactively anchor an arbitrary
prefix, and path members outside the seed remain ordinary continuation.

Static paths, insufficient keyframe density/span, competing paths and paths
whose support is concentrated in two distant frames remain unresolved. The
bootstrap never interpolates or creates a coordinate.

## Foam material identity

Raw eligible Foam/material evidence may form an observation-only material
track before Oil authority. This track has no publication authority. It may
identify that a material-path row continues a Foam/residue boundary and apply
candidate-specific opposition to reuse of that same track as Oil.

Material identity follows bounded Y/source/shape/texture continuity rather than
a fixed last-Foam coordinate or an unbounded timer. A distinct lower Oil
proposal is preserved in candidate admission. Foam-derived opposition cannot
veto unrelated ordinary Oil or create the lower coordinate.

Registered dynamic material-path anchor authority requires low candidate-local
material texture conflict. High conflict demotes the route to candidate-only;
it does not hard-delete the proposal. Other anchor routes must report and honor
their own material-identity gates rather than silently bypassing this result.

## Historical and legacy boundary

D1–D4 current-frame algorithms may remain evidence providers after extraction.
Historical D5 Foam-to-Oil routing is not restored. Unreachable pre-cutover
selection modules are retired after production/test/diagnostic reachability is
proved absent; Git history remains their provenance.

Version-prefixed flags may remain in exported trace compatibility, but new
internal control flow uses semantic names. R11 introduces no Base/Accum,
filename, timestamp or private-video branch.

## Performance and migration

Structural commits preserve candidate/output fingerprints and debug-disabled
timing within normal run variance. Intentional R11 changes receive new replay
fingerprints and causal evidence. The reset is complete when production has one
final authority owner, authority reasons are observable, bootstrap and material
identity are independently testable, and central orchestration no longer owns
their decision details.
