## Problem and reuse boundary

The [intake verification](../60-evidence/s11/2026-10-12-program-review-intake.md)
reproduces current peer support, including zero support on both correct and wrong
continuations. Cross-family proximity takes the maximum eligible peer contribution;
it is not multiple independent votes or sufficient evidence of liquid-interface
role. Public lower-range candidates survive geometry acquisition but lack this
support, while an upper non-target receives anchor authority. Foam also loses
the reviewed upper before episode publication. These are distinct failure paths.

Keep geometry, role and temporal identity separate. A model may describe current
native observations; it cannot create a boundary, choose a replacement Y, transfer
a regional label to an entire path, or certify continuity from similarity alone.
Any later role contradiction must be evaluated at both anchor and continuation
consumption, including downstream owner consequences. Do not repair only one
authority route, require nonzero legacy support everywhere, or relax Foam episodes.

Reuse the current owners rather than building a second detector:

| Responsibility | Existing owner / proposed boundary |
|---|---|
| Source and fixed-center geometry | Existing native captures, `oil_material_path.diagnose_center_path_alternatives` and `tests/diagnostics/s11_contour_contact_probe.py`; P1 remains offline and supplies no role authority |
| Candidate/evidence join | Original frame/index/source/Y and A1 lineage; retain all candidates, including unavailable and duplicate views |
| Offline role operation | A separate optional diagnostic adapter is justified only for the pretrained dependency; reuse existing geometry and evaluation records |
| Typed shadow | Existing Witness sidecar/validation; explicit versioned delta before adding role/presence fields; no resolver-facing metric |
| Oil consumption | `oil_candidate_authority.py`, `oil_phase_identity.py`, `oil_observation_resolver.py` after O2/V5 and separate O3 |
| Temporal ownership | Existing tracklet/lifecycle/selector owners under separate O4 acceptance |
| Foam | Shared current pixels/geometry, independent presence and upper-position evidence; existing raw-front and episode owners |
| Publication | Existing composition, selected-candidate provenance, CSV/events/report; no synthesized measurements |

## CR-1 operation to freeze before execution

### Adopted candidate-centred context scope

After the [reference audit](../50-diagnostics/s11/2026-10-12-cr1-reference-preflight.md),
the user selected its recommended regional-context comparison. CR-1 may therefore
sample around the original candidate scalar or previously reviewed context marker
even when no candidate-owned contour exists. These are **sampling anchors**, not
generated horizontal boundaries or new position truth. Keep the original scalar,
fractional floor/ceil alternatives, source index and actual review precision.
Do not snap an anchor to an edge, fill a reviewed interval, or label its surrounding
pixels as pure material. The output is `regional_context_preference`; it does not
certify a contour role, target localization, continuity or production support.

Reuse the existing S1 Oil-vicinity, rim-seed and internal-texture contexts as one
fixed three-exemplar bank at their existing precision. They are not pure masks
and none is a qualified Foam-upper prototype. Keep Foam upper/presence and public
interval-only cases explicitly unassessed where this bank cannot support that
claim. Freeze common relative strip support, full/local geometry, source/episode
exclusion and the real positive/opposition screen before any model scores. This
limited scope does not require new source labeling and does not change the later
native-support/physical-role acceptance requirements.

### Fixed encoder and descriptor

Test one fixed DINOv2 ViT-S/14 encoder, no training/fine-tuning, one reference bank,
and full-context versus local-context representations. Use candidate-conditioned
upper/lower strip vectors rather than one global vector shared by every candidate:
`z = normalize(concat(u, v, normalize(u - v)))`. Similarity is a normalized-vector
inner product, not a calibrated probability. Keep the supplied unique-maximum
preference rule, `1e-6` tie rule and explicit unavailable reasons for diagnostic
ranking only. `SUPPORTED / CONTRADICTED / UNASSESSED` need a later independently
frozen operating policy; a unique nearest prototype does not supply that policy.

The [official model card](https://github.com/facebookresearch/dinov2/blob/main/MODEL_CARD.md)
describes 14-pixel patches and 384-dimensional ViT-S embeddings. The
[official repository](https://github.com/facebookresearch/dinov2#license) provides
the DINOv2 code/weights under Apache 2.0. This is a plausible feature backend,
not evidence of compressor role accuracy or target-Windows cost. Pin the exact
DINOv2 variant, code revision, weights and dependency/license inventory at preflight;
do not inherit the terms or behavior of unrelated backbones now in that repository.

Token overlap is a risk to measure, not a claim that the model cannot distinguish
nearby boundaries. If the chosen support cannot represent a requested comparison,
record that limitation before ranking. The local-context intervention can itself
change model statistics; disclose that limitation rather than claiming a perfect
causal isolation of attention/context.

Metadata exclusion does not remove visual scene memorization. Separate prototype
self-reproduction from candidate competition and source transfer. A one-prototype
feasibility result is not calibration, a generalization estimate or an operating
point. Similar descriptors from the same pixels remain one evidence lineage.

## Local footprint and removal contract

Use one ignored root, `sample/output/s11-cr1-workspace/`, with experiment-owned
`env/`, `models/`, `cache/` and `runs/` directories. Keep a manifest of every owned
path, dependency/model version and source hash. Direct installer and model caches
to this root; avoid global installs, shared-cache mutation and duplicated
environments/data per worktree. Reuse existing source captures by reference.
The optional diagnostic adapter is the only inference entry: application imports,
default dependencies and packaging remain unchanged. Install the minimum needed
dependencies instead of copying the entire GUI environment. No training,
fine-tuning, automatic model updates or runtime downloads are part of CR-1.

The additional local experiment footprint has a **3,000,000,000-byte maximum**.
Plan up to 2 GB for environment, weights and download/temporary caches and up to
1 GB for run artifacts. Measure allocated disk usage, also record logical bytes,
and include every CR-1-owned copy outside the root in the total; moving files or
using a shared cache does not evade the cap. Check space before installation,
downloads and result writes, with a conservative bound for temporary/unpacked
files. If the next operation cannot stay within the budget, stop and record the
limitation rather than silently raising it. The cap is a constraint, not a
verified installation-size estimate. Future Windows packages need separate
measurement and are not created during this local experiment.

Stream features through inference and discard full token maps after scoring by
default. Retain the complete candidate/role score table, unavailable reasons,
input/configuration/model hashes and the evidence needed to assess the conclusion.
Bound image capture by a declared review roster; report all failures and ambiguous
cases in the tables even when their full image dumps are omitted. If necessary
evidence cannot fit, stop rather than dropping cases or truncating denominators.
Keep one pinned model/environment set; do not accumulate per-run model copies,
full-frame dumps, feature histories or obsolete packages. Recalculation cost is
an explicit tradeoff for not retaining large intermediate arrays.

Use CPU as the recorded baseline on Mac. Measure cold/warm wall time, peak RSS,
per-frame/per-candidate cost, saved-input versus decoder-inclusive cost and disk
growth. Any later MPS trial is a separately identified acceleration comparison;
it neither replaces CPU evidence nor predicts Windows CPU performance. A fixed
report layout should expose changed, failed and ambiguous cases without requiring
manual review of a fresh full image dump on every run.

Before the full comparison, verify that a smoke run writes only to declared paths
and that the removal set contains no shared environment, source media or retained
evidence. On rejection/closure, preserve the compact complete readout, settings,
versions/hashes and judgment-bearing evidence in the existing evidence/diagnostic
owners, then remove the experiment-owned environment, weights, caches and
reproducible intermediates. Retire or revert the optional adapter where unused;
keep deliberate Git decision history without committing model binaries. Verify
the final path inventory, reclaimed bytes, application entry/dependency invariance
and surviving evidence. A useful result may retain one pinned set within the cap;
production integration still follows the separate acceptance sequence.

**User retention override (October 12):** keep the current CR-1 environment,
weights and caches regardless of the research verdict. Remove them only when
the user explicitly requests environment cleanup. This overrides the automatic
removal clause above for this experiment, without raising the 3 GB cap or
authorizing additional model/environment copies. Close completed managed runs
with explicit evidence retention; run closure is distinct from environment removal.

**October 13 cleanup refinement:** the user's request to remove unnecessary
material permits retiring unused installer metadata. Retain one pinned runtime,
model/source set and the small prepared inputs for the adopted post-CR1 policy
investigation. Saved-score design and verification do not need encoder execution;
qualified new-input calibration/shadow work still needs the fixed representation.
Reassess this set when that policy is rejected, replaced or no longer needs local
inference. Retention does not imply production adoption. The
[cleanup evidence](../60-evidence/s11/2026-10-13-post-cr1-review-intake.md#추가-정리와-cr1-환경-판단)
records the removal scope and surviving checks; historical receipts remain frozen.

## Progression and stop conditions

1. Verify the preceding repository cleanup, freeze WP1, and satisfy footprint/
   isolation readiness before the scored comparison, or record exactly why a
   role cannot yet be evaluated. Reuse closed source judgments; do not start a
   new source-label collection by default.
2. Verify arithmetic and measured encoder/geometry reachability, including actual
   missing/tie cases. Then run the frozen positive/opposition screen; stop at the
   first failed progression condition, preserving outputs and the reason.
3. If eligible, run the frozen full candidate/control comparison once. Keep known
   positives, known wrong selections and unreviewed alternatives separate. A/B
   improvement is not established by suppressing a sentinel in isolation.
4. Only a useful role result proceeds to operating-policy development and frozen
   typed shadow, V2/V3/V4, then V5 Windows shadow/O2. O3 consumer acceptance and O4
   owner/handoff work remain separate. Final integrated field/report evaluation
   remains P6/O5; there is no shortcut from a public prototype to behavior.

The initial budget is one encoder/bank and two representations. The source's
4–6 engineer-day estimate is a planning assumption, not an execution commitment
or permission to tune until a gate passes. Close CR-1 at the frozen limit if it
fails; partial-role usefulness and unassessable roles stay separate. Do not change
the bank, thresholds, cases or model after seeing results under the same trial.

The user subsequently adopted a complete-candidate usefulness review after the
first frozen trial stopped: its full and local representations both passed the

occur. Preserve that first preflight, readout and failed progression result.
They answer the narrow context-benefit screen, not the utility of all pretrained

not retrospective promotion or a tuned replacement for that trial.

Reuse the same encoder, weights, bank, support, normalization and preference

complete the 164-record inventory once. Full/local differences remain explanatory
evidence, not a requirement that the local representation fail. Compare known
target preservation, known wrong-target preferences and competing unreviewed
alternatives with the saved application selections and unchanged mean diagnostic.
Their scores/operating rules differ; this is an outcome comparison on common
candidate identities, not a controlled feature-only comparison or a shared cutoff.

Keep reference-self, reference-neighbor and outside-reference-episode groups
separate. Explicitly tagged development-only scoring may include the excluded
42/44 s errors; it cannot enter the first trial's evaluation denominator or
establish generalization. Report all candidates, missing/censored results,
duplicate coordinates and unreviewed alternatives. Never count an unreviewed
alternative as correct merely because a known wrong target loses preference.

Review completion requires the complete planned readout/control results, preserved
first-trial verdict, source/role limitations, bounded resource measurements and
an explained disposition: worth further validation, useful only within a stated
scope, no demonstrated utility, or insufficient evidence to decide. Preferential
scores alone cannot close O2 or grant a physical selector. If the readout reveals
a decision-bearing remaining ambiguity, state the smallest additional evidence
needed rather than tuning the fixed model. A partial result can justify a bounded
next study without becoming a product PASS.

Sequential consumer behavior, independent Foam-upper observation, Windows shadow
and final nine-segment/report acceptance remain later stages. The CR-1 review

## Post-CR1 operating-policy proposal

The [supplied follow-up](../70-reference/s11-post-cr1-review-2026-10-12/S11-program-review-and-work-spec-post-CR1-2026-10-12.md)
identifies information discarded by category-only comparison. Preserve continuous
support and role competition together. This is one development hypothesis over
the existing fixed representation, not a new encoder/bank search or an operating
point selected from the exposed 164 records. Calibration and evaluation entry are
owned by [Witness Validation](../30-validation/s11-interface-observability-witness-validation.md#post-cr1-operating-policy-and-consumer-acceptance).

### Observation and decision boundary

Extend the existing Witness/diagnostic owner through a reviewed versioned delta;
the following is a design contract, not an implemented API or a second sidecar:

| Content | Required distinction |
|---|---|
| Identity and lineage | Source/frame/actual timestamp, Glass/geometry/profile and bank digests; original candidate index/family/source-Y and available native support |
| Measured evidence | All full/local interface, structure and texture scores, signed margins, common support/visible columns/crop completeness and every required floor/ceil variant |
| Availability | Measured, numerical tie, unavailable and inconsistent variants remain distinct; include reasons. Missing evidence is neither a measured zero nor a physical negative |
| Policy provenance | Explicit UNCALIBRATED status until a policy/version/partition digest is qualified; similarities are not probabilities |
| Claim scope | Regional context, physical boundary role, native contour and fixed-X scalar are separate claims. Record only the precision actually supported |

Keep `decision=NOT_EVALUATED` on uncalibrated role observations. A later calibrated
output uses the existing `INTERFACE_SUPPORTED / INTERNAL_OR_ARTIFACT / UNRESOLVED`
vocabulary at its qualified scope. Failure of positive eligibility cannot emit
`INTERNAL_OR_ARTIFACT`: that needs separately validated negative evidence. A
regional preference alone cannot emit a boundary-role assertion.

### Single local-support policy family

For candidate `c` and its frozen required anchor variants `V(c)`, define:

```text
A(c) = min over v in V(c) of local_interface_similarity(v)
M(v) = local_interface_similarity(v)
       - max(local_structure_similarity(v), local_texture_similarity(v))

provisional eligibility = required support/geometry available for every v
                         AND M(v) > 1e-6 for every v
                         AND A(c) >= tau_support
```

`tau_support` remains unset. Freeze its selection procedure and acceptance scope
before calibration. `1e-6` retains CR-1's numerical tie convention; it is not a
physical confidence margin. Do not add a large margin floor, discard an
unfavorable/missing fractional variant or substitute a favorable rounded anchor.
No eligibility decision can be executed with an unset operating point.

Keep full scores for context/opposition analysis without a universal full-texture
veto. Full/local/mean are dependent views, not independent votes. A higher score
than a reviewed target does not identify an unreviewed competitor; preserve the
whole candidate population and unresolved same-boundary versus other-role claims.
No maximum-score winner or proximity-based label transfer is implied.

The [October 13 clarification](../50-diagnostics/s11/2026-10-13-cr1-competitor-role.md)
separates physical description from practical acceptance. The user identifies
fine Foam above the lower red marks and accepts B/idx8/Y841 as an approximation
because of detector limitations. The original same-physical-boundary interpretation
is superseded. B's local texture preference conflicts with that practical allowance;
it is not a demonstrated physical-interface false negative. A/idx19/Y836 retains
its historical label and positive local margin, but its current qualification as
a physical alternative is not established by these replies.

Preserve two independent evaluation facets: physical role/localization evidence
and user-accepted practical approximation, with their scope and uncertainty. Do
not discard B's practical acceptance or silently demand perfect-pixel accuracy;
do not count it as clean physical-role calibration truth either. No exact-X truth,
error band, front/back projection relation or universal proxy rule follows from
the red marks. Evaluate downstream movement/report usefulness separately. Do not
relax the sign or invent an argmax route to rescue this one case; any subsequent
operating-policy comparison must disclose which facet it measures.

The existing sample4 104-to-224 transform gives a 6.5-native-pixel token spacing;
it cannot certify a native or subpixel boundary coordinate. Before transfer,
freeze the applicable geometry/scale, support and source/ROI transform. Those
choices, independent calibration/evaluation sources, role-label precision,
resource bounds and permissible residual impact remain executable-preflight
requirements, not values inferred from this proposal. Numeric publication keeps
the selected current native candidate Y. A future fixed-X representation needs
its own candidate/support lineage; no post-selection snapping or reference Y.

### Executable saved-contract preflight

[The offline CR1 adapter](../../tests/diagnostics/s11_contextual_role_preflight.py)
uses the existing O2 hash, validation and write-once helpers. The distinct adapter
is needed because CR1 records carry full/local role vectors and floor/ceil anchors,
not the O1 sector-score envelope. It does not modify the frozen CR1 runner,

Its versioned [input manifest](../50-diagnostics/s11/2026-10-13-post-cr1-policy-preflight/manifest.json)

where present, existing O1/A1 native support. Candidate representative Y, a native

support availability never certifies its physical role.

The readout preserves complete score/variant/support evidence through hashed
JSON pointers to the existing table rather than duplicating it. Derived minimum
support and signed margins are measurements only. Ties, disagreement, partial
availability and unavailable measurements remain distinct; malformed provenance,

tolerance is separate from the `1e-6` preference tie rule. All decisions remain
`NOT_EVALUATED`, including when both measurements and native geometry exist.

The calibration procedure enumerates support breakpoints only after suitable
calibration inputs are independently frozen. Compare positive loss, wrong-target
acceptance by reviewed family and unresolved/availability denominators; retain
the tradeoff frontier for one bounded operating-point review. Impact bounds and
the point itself must be qualified before evaluation. This preflight neither
enumerates those breakpoints nor relabels exposed sources as holdout. It supplies
no negative classifier, model inference, default dependency or production shadow.
Its [completed contract checks and remaining question](../50-diagnostics/s11/2026-10-13-post-cr1-policy-preflight.md)

The same adapter's optional `--review-manifest` path binds the subsequent

## Independent Foam path and future consumption

Foam presence, upper localization and temporal disappearance answer different
questions. A visible layer with a cropped/unresolved upper remains present with
unavailable height; rejecting a front does not establish physical absence.
The proposed fields stay diagnostic until their schema and consumer are reviewed.
Shared image features cannot make final Oil a Foam prerequisite or final Foam an
Oil mask. Oil role feasibility and Foam role feasibility can advance independently.

WP3 first covers P5-a observation feasibility. Its P5-b raw-front integration
requires its own parent/Foam behavior contract; any typed Witness consumer also
requires O2/O3. WP8/P5-c opens only when a source-qualified correct raw upper is
demonstrably lost downstream. Presence-driven episodes would change the input
semantics and require a separate design, not a persistence-threshold adjustment.

The post-CR1 WP07/WP08 aliases retain this order. Reuse existing edge/path/support
owners to establish an independent current native upper before raw-front
integration. Internal Foam texture in the CR-1 bank is not an upper-front
prototype. A learned Foam-upper bank/operation needs its own frozen experiment;
the Oil policy provides no implicit authorization or evidence for it. Preserve
dark water Foam A, sample4/public uppers, internal texture/rim, no-Foam B/C,
cropped beer upper and unclear milk lower at their actual review precision.
