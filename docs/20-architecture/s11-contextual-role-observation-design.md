## Problem and reuse boundary

The [intake verification](../60-evidence/s11/2026-10-12-program-review-intake.md)
reproduces current peer support, including zero support on both correct and wrong
continuations. Cross-family proximity takes the maximum eligible peer contribution;
it is not multiple independent votes or sufficient evidence of liquid-interface
role. Public lower-range candidates survive geometry acquisition but lack this
support, while an upper non-target receives anchor authority. Foam also loses
the reviewed upper before episode publication. These are distinct failure paths.

Keep geometry, role and temporal identity separate. A model may describe current
native observations; it cannot create a boundary, choose a replacement Y, transfer
a regional label to an entire path, or certify continuity from similarity alone.
Any later role contradiction must be evaluated at both anchor and continuation
consumption, including downstream owner consequences. Do not repair only one
authority route, require nonzero legacy support everywhere, or relax Foam episodes.

Reuse the current owners rather than building a second detector:

| Responsibility | Existing owner / proposed boundary |
|---|---|
| Source and fixed-center geometry | Existing native captures, `oil_material_path.diagnose_center_path_alternatives` and `tests/diagnostics/s11_contour_contact_probe.py`; P1 remains offline and supplies no role authority |
| Candidate/evidence join | Original frame/index/source/Y and A1 lineage; retain all candidates, including unavailable and duplicate views |
| Offline role operation | A separate optional diagnostic adapter is justified only for the pretrained dependency; reuse existing geometry and evaluation records |
| Typed shadow | Existing Witness sidecar/validation; explicit versioned delta before adding role/presence fields; no resolver-facing metric |
| Oil consumption | `oil_candidate_authority.py`, `oil_phase_identity.py`, `oil_observation_resolver.py` after O2/V5 and separate O3 |
| Temporal ownership | Existing tracklet/lifecycle/selector owners under separate O4 acceptance |
| Foam | Shared current pixels/geometry, independent presence and upper-position evidence; existing raw-front and episode owners |
| Publication | Existing composition, selected-candidate provenance, CSV/events/report; no synthesized measurements |

## CR-1 operation to freeze before execution

### Adopted candidate-centred context scope

After the [reference audit](../50-diagnostics/s11/2026-10-12-cr1-reference-preflight.md),
the user selected its recommended regional-context comparison. CR-1 may therefore
sample around the original candidate scalar or previously reviewed context marker
even when no candidate-owned contour exists. These are **sampling anchors**, not
generated horizontal boundaries or new position truth. Keep the original scalar,
fractional floor/ceil alternatives, source index and actual review precision.
Do not snap an anchor to an edge, fill a reviewed interval, or label its surrounding
pixels as pure material. The output is `regional_context_preference`; it does not
certify a contour role, target localization, continuity or production support.

Reuse the existing S1 Oil-vicinity, rim-seed and internal-texture contexts as one
fixed three-exemplar bank at their existing precision. They are not pure masks
and none is a qualified Foam-upper prototype. Keep Foam upper/presence and public
interval-only cases explicitly unassessed where this bank cannot support that
claim. Freeze common relative strip support, full/local geometry, source/episode
exclusion and the real positive/opposition screen before any model scores. This
limited scope does not require new source labeling and does not change the later
native-support/physical-role acceptance requirements.

### Fixed encoder and descriptor

Test one fixed DINOv2 ViT-S/14 encoder, no training/fine-tuning, one reference bank,
and full-context versus local-context representations. Use candidate-conditioned
upper/lower strip vectors rather than one global vector shared by every candidate:
`z = normalize(concat(u, v, normalize(u - v)))`. Similarity is a normalized-vector
inner product, not a calibrated probability. Keep the supplied unique-maximum
preference rule, `1e-6` tie rule and explicit unavailable reasons for diagnostic
ranking only. `SUPPORTED / CONTRADICTED / UNASSESSED` need a later independently
frozen operating policy; a unique nearest prototype does not supply that policy.

The [official model card](https://github.com/facebookresearch/dinov2/blob/main/MODEL_CARD.md)
describes 14-pixel patches and 384-dimensional ViT-S embeddings. The
[official repository](https://github.com/facebookresearch/dinov2#license) provides
the DINOv2 code/weights under Apache 2.0. This is a plausible feature backend,
not evidence of compressor role accuracy or target-Windows cost. Pin the exact
DINOv2 variant, code revision, weights and dependency/license inventory at preflight;
do not inherit the terms or behavior of unrelated backbones now in that repository.

using a shared cache does not evade the cap. Check space before installation,

## Progression and stop conditions

1. Verify the preceding repository cleanup, freeze WP1, and satisfy footprint/
   isolation readiness before the scored comparison, or record exactly why a
   role cannot yet be evaluated. Reuse closed source judgments; do not start a
   new source-label collection by default.
2. Verify arithmetic and measured encoder/geometry reachability, including actual
   missing/tie cases. Then run the frozen positive/opposition screen; stop at the
   first failed progression condition, preserving outputs and the reason.
3. If eligible, run the frozen full candidate/control comparison once. Keep known
   positives, known wrong selections and unreviewed alternatives separate. A/B
   improvement is not established by suppressing a sentinel in isolation.
4. Only a useful role result proceeds to operating-policy development and frozen
   typed shadow, V2/V3/V4, then V5 Windows shadow/O2. O3 consumer acceptance and O4
   owner/handoff work remain separate. Final integrated field/report evaluation
   remains P6/O5; there is no shortcut from a public prototype to behavior.

The initial budget is one encoder/bank and two representations. The source's
4–6 engineer-day estimate is a planning assumption, not an execution commitment
or permission to tune until a gate passes. Close CR-1 at the frozen limit if it
fails; partial-role usefulness and unassessable roles stay separate. Do not change
the bank, thresholds, cases or model after seeing results under the same trial.

## Post-CR1 operating-policy proposal

The [supplied follow-up](../70-reference/s11-post-cr1-review-2026-10-12/S11-program-review-and-work-spec-post-CR1-2026-10-12.md)
identifies information discarded by category-only comparison. Preserve continuous
support and role competition together. This is one development hypothesis over
the existing fixed representation, not a new encoder/bank search or an operating
point selected from the exposed 164 records. Calibration and evaluation entry are
owned by [Witness Validation](../30-validation/s11-interface-observability-witness-validation.md#post-cr1-operating-policy-and-consumer-acceptance).

### Observation and decision boundary

Extend the existing Witness/diagnostic owner through a reviewed versioned delta;
the following is a design contract, not an implemented API or a second sidecar:

| Content | Required distinction |
|---|---|
| Identity and lineage | Source/frame/actual timestamp, Glass/geometry/profile and bank digests; original candidate index/family/source-Y and available native support |
| Measured evidence | All full/local interface, structure and texture scores, signed margins, common support/visible columns/crop completeness and every required floor/ceil variant |
| Availability | Measured, numerical tie, unavailable and inconsistent variants remain distinct; include reasons. Missing evidence is neither a measured zero nor a physical negative |
| Policy provenance | Explicit UNCALIBRATED status until a policy/version/partition digest is qualified; similarities are not probabilities |
| Claim scope | Regional context, physical boundary role, native contour and fixed-X scalar are separate claims. Record only the precision actually supported |

Keep `decision=NOT_EVALUATED` on uncalibrated role observations. A later calibrated
output uses the existing `INTERFACE_SUPPORTED / INTERNAL_OR_ARTIFACT / UNRESOLVED`
vocabulary at its qualified scope. Failure of positive eligibility cannot emit
`INTERNAL_OR_ARTIFACT`: that needs separately validated negative evidence. A
regional preference alone cannot emit a boundary-role assertion.

### Single local-support policy family

For candidate `c` and its frozen required anchor variants `V(c)`, define:

```text
A(c) = min over v in V(c) of local_interface_similarity(v)
M(v) = local_interface_similarity(v)
       - max(local_structure_similarity(v), local_texture_similarity(v))

provisional eligibility = required support/geometry available for every v
                         AND M(v) > 1e-6 for every v
                         AND A(c) >= tau_support
```

`tau_support` remains unset. Freeze its selection procedure and acceptance scope
before calibration. `1e-6` retains CR-1's numerical tie convention; it is not a
physical confidence margin. Do not add a large margin floor, discard an
unfavorable/missing fractional variant or substitute a favorable rounded anchor.
No eligibility decision can be executed with an unset operating point.

Keep full scores for context/opposition analysis without a universal full-texture
veto. Full/local/mean are dependent views, not independent votes. A higher score
than a reviewed target does not identify an unreviewed competitor; preserve the
whole candidate population and unresolved same-boundary versus other-role claims.
No maximum-score winner or proximity-based label transfer is implied.

The [October 13 clarification](../50-diagnostics/s11/2026-10-13-cr1-competitor-role.md)
separates physical description from practical acceptance. The user identifies
fine Foam above the lower red marks and accepts B/idx8/Y841 as an approximation

Preserve two independent evaluation facets: physical role/localization evidence

not discard B's practical acceptance or silently demand perfect-pixel accuracy;
do not count it as clean physical-role calibration truth either. No exact-X truth,

the red marks. Evaluate downstream movement/report usefulness separately. Do not
relax the sign or invent an argmax route to rescue this one case; any subsequent
operating-policy comparison must disclose which facet it measures.

The existing sample4 104-to-224 transform gives a 6.5-native-pixel token spacing;
