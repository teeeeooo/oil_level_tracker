# S11 Foam/Spatial Authority Repair Architecture

**Status:** `ACTIVE — S11-R2 implementation and validation`

## Purpose

This document owns the bounded S11-R2 detector change that lets stronger existing Spatial proof challenge a weak accepted Oil boundary under authoritative Foam topology. It is a responsibility repair, not a new detector family or a general threshold-loosening pass.

The causal record is [`../50-diagnostics/s11/s11-r2-foam-spatial-authority-diagnostic.md`](../50-diagnostics/s11/s11-r2-foam-spatial-authority-diagnostic.md). Acceptance is owned by [`../30-validation/s11-foam-spatial-authority-repair-validation.md`](../30-validation/s11-foam-spatial-authority-repair-validation.md).

## Existing ownership preserved

The production flow remains:

`current-frame evidence -> typed semantic result -> canonical boundary -> serialized temporal owner -> public Oil Y`

- D5 Foam topology constrains physically eligible Oil hypotheses.
- Spatial supplies bounded current-frame corroboration only.
- the typed/canonical current-frame result remains the sole numeric publication authority;
- the serialized reducer remains the sole temporal owner.

Spatial does not publish independently, invent missing observations or use future frames.

## R2 arbitration contract

An already accepted Oil boundary may request a Spatial challenger only when all of the following are true:

1. an authoritative accepted-Foam component mask exists;
2. the incumbent's `artifact_likelihood - boundary_likelihood` is at least `0.20`;
3. the incumbent fails the existing Spatial path contract;
4. the challenger is produced by the existing relative-evidence and full-path selection routes with their existing hard-safety and weak `5/5` requirements;
5. the challenger's `boundary_likelihood - artifact_likelihood` exceeds the incumbent's corresponding margin by at least `0.08`.

Only then may the canonical current-frame frame be rebuilt around the challenger. Otherwise the accepted incumbent remains unchanged; failed arbitration must not turn it into ambiguity.

This is deliberately asymmetric. A well-supported incumbent does not reopen competition, and ordinary non-Foam scenes retain the exact prior pipeline.

## Path-invalid indirect authority repair

Under authoritative accepted-Foam context, a preliminary relative boundary that fails its own Spatial path loses both direct and indirect stopping authority. The fallback must continue to the existing spatially corroborated textured-boundary selector, which can choose a distinct candidate only under its already accepted local tie, paired-edge and cross-ROI rules. Outside accepted-Foam context the prior stopping behavior is preserved; this prevents low-exposure ambiguity from acquiring a new general recovery route.

This mirrors the existing S11 principle that a hard-invalid candidate cannot remain a comparison anchor after losing direct authority. It does not allow the path-invalid preliminary boundary itself to publish.

## Foam-separated support floor

The D5 Foam-separated narrow candidate requires horizontal material coverage of at least `0.30`, replacing `0.20`. This rejects short bottom structures demonstrated in real-video replay while retaining broad material-interface fixtures and candidates.

No other D5 score, geometry, Foam-front distance or topology threshold changes.

## Invariants

R2 must preserve all of the following:

- positive no-interface, unavailable evidence, severe glare/exclusion/border conflict and authoritative Foam topology remain fail-closed;
- weak Spatial recovery still requires the existing full `5/5` cross-ROI proof;
- paired-edge, sector count, local-authority tie and scalar semantic thresholds are unchanged;
- no video, frame, Recipe, truth or repository-sample identity is available to production decisions;
- no optical flow, raster history, interpolation or graph-derived feedback is added;
- only finite canonical detector observations reach tracking, CSV, overlay and report data;
- report bridges remain display-only and cannot compensate for a detector miss.

## Non-goals

R2 is not intended to maximize raw numeric coverage, guarantee 50% corpus publication, solve overlay-dominated `base_sample_1`, or convert visually unclear sample3 intervals into numeric Oil. A lower misleading-extrema rate with a small net coverage gain is a valid improvement because the product objective is a report that communicates the actual observed Glass flow.

## Source ownership

- `oil_shadow_observations.py` owns the Foam-separated material-support floor.
- `oil_spatial_fallback.py` owns incumbent eligibility, path validation and challenger comparison.
- `oil_shadow_pipeline.py` owns invoking the bounded challenger route in accepted-Foam context while retaining one current-frame result.
- the existing report architecture owns presentation only and receives no new estimation authority.
