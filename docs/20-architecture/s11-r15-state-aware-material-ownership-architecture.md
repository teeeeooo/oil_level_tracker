# S11-R15 State-Aware Material Ownership Architecture

## Purpose

R15 replaces R14's remaining post-path and cross-material ambiguities. One
detector must still support Base and Accum without private-video timestamps,
pixel bands, Glass identifiers or source-name authority.

## Oil component admission and continuation

A confirmed `EMPTY_NO_INTERFACE` prior is context for the completed window,
not a coordinate. Before the first numeric Oil publication, a component must
enter through the lower analysis-area band and show bounded upward progress in
at least three same-frame observations. A nearly fixed lower structure remains
provisional. Once entry is proven, later components use the ordinary component
and UNKNOWN-handoff rules.

Oil-to-Oil transitions still require the same non-empty component identifier.
Changing component requires an UNKNOWN observation followed by a new
independent anchor. Continuation bounding no longer uses a fixed distance from
the nearest anchor for a one-sided tail. It keeps a same-component tail only
while every intervening row retains trajectory support, registered Oil-band
motion, registered coverage and final confidence. The first broken row ends
the extension; repetition or source identity cannot restart it.

Every numeric Oil remains an eligible candidate from that exact frame. Initial
state, component continuity and registered motion never synthesize, carry or
interpolate a coordinate.

## Foam and Oil material ownership

Foam episode resolution consumes the final Oil result produced by the Oil
resolver. A published Oil row aliases Foam only when the two rows are within
the symmetric same-boundary tolerance. A larger negative separation is
inverted topology, not alias. A positive separation above tolerance is a
distinct lower Oil interface.

Unselected Oil proposals are not public composition authority and cannot hard
reject a coherent dynamic Foam episode. The R14 candidate-only alias screen
and prior-alias continuation track are removed. Coincident candidate-level Oil
evidence remains available to the Oil-side Foam-material opposition owner.

## Detached Foam material phenotype

Foam material has one component-shape classifier with three outcomes:
bottom-connected material, detached layer and detached droplet. A detached
droplet is a compact, non-structural, sufficiently large bright or chromatic
material component. It is only sequence-eligible with registered internal and
adjacent dynamic support. This does not relax static, glare, persistence or
episode requirements and does not promote a fixed bright blob.

The previous detached-layer-only helper and duplicate width/fill admission
branches are replaced by the shared phenotype result.

## Observability

Foam trace projection owns Foam-specific stages. It must not report Oil
top-k/authority stages for Foam candidates. Trace data distinguishes eligible,
unconfirmed, static-rejected, Oil-alias-rejected and confirmed Foam; alias
diagnostics include relation, matched Oil Y and signed separation.

Final numeric counts continue to use final resolved kind/source Y and never a
candidate's intermediate `selected` bit.
