# S11-R7 Evidence-Tiered Trajectory Validation Contract

**Status:** `LOCAL PASS — SECURE WINDOWS HOLDOUT PENDING`

## Oracle and comparison

The oracle is direct source-frame review inside the configured Glass ROI,
supported by checked truth/provisional annotations and controlled latent-cause
pairs. R6 is the field-failed comparison. R4/R5/R6 exact counts and fingerprints
are historical evidence, not acceptance targets.

## Gate 1 — authority cleanup

Tests and source inspection must prove:

1. changing only a current-frame candidate's `selected`, temporal confidence,
   margin, tracker action or reason cannot change R7 final selection;
2. final authority is one of hard-invalid, candidate-only,
   continuation-eligible or anchor-eligible;
3. an ambiguous weak selected shadow cannot become an anchor;
4. direct-anchor and cluster support do not duplicate one evidence source;
5. every numeric coordinate belongs to an eligible same-frame candidate; and
6. current-frame temporal fields remain debug/preview only.

## Gate 2 — Oil anchor and continuation mechanics

Controlled sequences must prove:

- weak ambiguous evidence cannot start or reacquire a path;
- strong boundary-dominant evidence forms a multi-frame anchor cluster;
- a registered dynamic material path may anchor despite high scalar ambiguity;
- a pixel-identical reflection twin remains non-numeric;
- continuation candidates between compatible anchor clusters remain numeric
  without interpolation;
- a frame with no candidate stays non-numeric;
- an unsupported long one-sided continuation decays to UNKNOWN;
- stationary real Oil with direct phase proof remains observable; and
- EMPTY/FULL transition topology cannot lock out later independent anchors.

## Gate 3 — initial-state retrospective semantics

Prove symmetric FULL/descending and EMPTY/rising cases:

- current-run confirmation plus at least two anchor-grade observations with
  compatible direction can infer the leading prefix even when the first catch
  is already mid-Glass;
- inferred state is separately persisted and leaves observed samples/coverage
  unchanged;
- ordinary UNKNOWN and rejected/raw Foam do not block inference;
- contradictory image-supported state produces conflict/no prefix;
- continuation-only or false single-boundary evidence cannot confirm the prior;
- no numeric coordinate is synthesized; and
- R7 streams are no longer globally returned as `NOT_APPLICABLE`.

## Gate 4 — Foam episodes

Controlled Foam validation must include:

- constant high-score glare;
- static glare with one registered motion spike;
- global exposure change and subpixel mask jitter;
- one droplet;
- coherent multi-frame dynamic Foam;
- a static prelude followed by genuine onset; and
- Oil plus confirmed Foam composition.

The first four publish no Foam. Genuine dynamic episodes remain, their static
prelude is not backfilled, and Foam never changes Oil selection.

## Gate 5 — event and report provenance

Prove:

- extrema use anchor-grade Oil only;
- continuation-only points cannot create a false high/low after a gap;
- trend events require an anchor-supported run and are not backdated through
  missing observations;
- report captures use the same trusted event sample;
- initial inferred FULL/EMPTY is visible as interpretation, not numeric Oil;
- solid/continuation/dashed presentation remains attributable; and
- CSV never gains an interpolated or prior-derived coordinate.

## Gate 6 — checked-in corpus

Replay the four fixed local video windows with matching Recipe, static
preparation, production detector, completed-window owner and report.

Directly review representative frames for every anchor cluster, long
continuation, Foam episode, state transition and extremum. Record candidate-lattice
recall separately from final publication so a representation miss cannot be
mistaken for a scoring miss.

Required preservation:

- sample2 clear stationary Oil remains continuously observable;
- sample3 `30–60 s` communicates EMPTY/inflow/Foam/full without a fixed-cap
  trajectory;
- sample3 black/reframe barrier remains unavailable;
- sample4 annotated Foam-absent interval publishes no Foam;
- Base encoded-overlay/unclear local frames are not forced numeric; and
- all numeric rows retain same-frame provenance.

No fixed coverage percentage is required. Acceptance requires better direct
image agreement and gap distribution without a new gross wrong-interface run.
