## Scope

This contract accepts or rejects the bounded design in [`../20-architecture/s11-sequence-observability-integrity-architecture.md`](../20-architecture/s11-sequence-observability-integrity-architecture.md). It supplements the accepted S11 detector baseline; it does not replace canonical Oil/Foam ownership, the serialized reducer or report provenance rules.

Acceptance is based on physical observation meaning. An unchanged four-video fingerprint or a larger numeric count is not a success criterion.

## Gate 1 — mechanism-level controlled regressions

### Dark border-cap integrity

Prove all sides of the conjunctive guard:

1. a near-top saturated dark phase with a materially brighter lower region is withheld from canonical, comparative and Spatial authority;
2. a near-bottom saturated dark phase with insufficient lower material area is also withheld, while the retained sample2 near-bottom Oil interface remains eligible;
3. moving the same transition into the interior leaves the cap guard inactive;
4. a near-border transition whose border phase is not saturated leaves the cap guard inactive;
5. existing bright-reversal and ordinary clear-boundary positives remain numeric; and
6. glare, collision, structural-line, paired-pulse and no-interface negatives remain non-numeric.

### Learned static Foam

Prove:

1. an accepted component dominated by the learned map receives `static_rejected` and publishes no Foam;
2. the same current-frame component remains eligible when no learned map exists or overlap is below `0.80`;
3. a component present in only one of three learning frames does not enter the map;
4. a persistent accepted component does enter the map deterministically;
5. reset-one/reset-all and multi-Glass isolation clear only the owned maps/states; and
6. current-frame score/mask diagnostics remain available even when publication is withheld.

### Foam layer coherence

Prove:

1. a broad component made from fragmented occupied rows receives `incoherent_rejected`;
2. an ordinary wide material layer remains eligible;
3. a narrow, compact partial layer remains eligible;
4. raw score/mask diagnostics survive publication rejection;
5. sample2 and the true sample3 inflow Foam episode remain published; and
6. blind Foam-absent sample3 drain frames do not create later Foam episodes.

## Gate 2 — sample3 transition contract

Run the production `OpenCvPhaseDetector`, matching Recipe, official static-artifact learning and one serialized owner at 2 FPS over `30.03–60.06 s`.

Use the checked-in blind provisional annotation only as a validation oracle, never a production input. The sequence contract is:

- preserve usable inflow observations and the user-confirmed Oil/Foam anchors around frames `900` and `1035` within the existing truth tolerance;
- preserve a bounded genuine Foam episode in the annotated inflow/agitation interval;
- publish no Foam episode in the blind Foam-absent drain interval beginning near `85 s`;
- on blind `full_like` representative frames `1200`, `1350`, `1500`, `1650` and `1800`, publish no numeric Oil on the upper dark Glass cap;
- after `42 s`, do not emit a false `DRAINING_VISIBLE` run sourced from that cap; and
- do not require `FULL_NO_INTERFACE` when current-frame evidence remains insufficient. `UNKNOWN_REVIEW` is preferable to a wrong numeric interface.

The test must fail if an old fingerprint reintroduces the demonstrated wrong full-state numerics.

## Gate 3 — corpus-wide effectiveness reconciliation

Replay the complete fixed 2 FPS qualification windows for `base_sample_1`, `sample2`, `sample3` and `sample4` with matching Recipes, static learning and serialized ownership.

Report per sample:

- numeric Oil count and longest missing span;
- confirmed-truth publication and raw Y error;
- Oil outcome/state distribution;
- Foam publication count and episode timing;
- every added and removed numeric Oil observation; and
- the reason each changed report landmark remains or disappears.

The retained `8/13` truth-near surface must not regress unless direct visual review proves an old truth expectation invalid. A lower aggregate numeric count is acceptable only for visually demonstrated wrong-interface removal. No unrelated sample may gain a persistent structure/reflection track.

## Gate 4 — negative-set audit

Run the complete current Oil/Foam preservation surface, including controlled and metamorphic brightness/contrast/noise neighborhoods. Classify each fixture by what it actually proves:

- observational equivalence / collision abstention;
- glare or exclusion hard safety;
- structural-line rejection;
- no-interface behavior;
- genuine Foam support;
- static-Foam publication opposition; or
- bounded temporal continuity.

Exact-output fixtures that encode a visually disproven sample3 full-state boundary must be updated to the new physical contract. Do not delete a negative merely because it fails; explain whether the implementation or the fixture expectation is wrong.

## Gate 5 — report semantic check

Regenerate report artifacts from the four replays and inspect the Glass crops and graphs. Verify that:

- sample3 no longer presents the upper cap as a measured plateau/drain;
- removed observations remain absent from CSV, cursor and overlay data;

- highest/lowest captures do not move onto a known Glass structure.

## Acceptance and stop rules

Accept only when all local gates pass and changes are explainable without identity branches. Stop or narrow if genuine Foam is suppressed, truth-near Oil is lost, a different structure becomes numeric, full-state ambiguity is merely relabeled as certain, or validation depends only on aggregate coverage.
