# S11-R4 Field Residual Authority Validation Contract

**Status:** `VALIDATING`

## Scope

This contract validates the bounded [R4 architecture](../20-architecture/s11-r4-field-residual-authority-architecture.md). Aggregate publication percentage is supporting evidence only; acceptance requires preserved physical meaning and the secure-Windows residual replay.

## Gate 1 — registered static-Foam controls

Prove:

1. unchanged exact overlap at `0.80` rejects;
2. a persistent accepted mask shifted within the bounded radius rejects through the conjunctive tolerant/reciprocal path;
3. exact overlap below `0.70`, tolerant coverage below `0.90` or reciprocal coverage below `0.70` remains insufficient independently;
4. a materially larger or partial new component is not rejected merely because it touches the learned map;
5. transient support in only one preparation frame remains unlearned;
6. shape mismatch fails explicitly; and
7. reset and multi-Glass lifecycle remain isolated.

## Gate 2 — strong onset confirmation

Prove:

1. the first strong sample is `persistence_pending` and publishes no Foam;
2. the second compatible strong sample publishes, and later compatible strong samples stay published;
3. an incompatible jump restarts confirmation;
4. weak, ambiguous, glare, static and incoherent outcomes clear the chain;
5. moderate-to-strong and strong-to-moderate transitions follow one compatible scalar chain: the current strong sample may confirm at two compatible observations, while a current moderate sample still requires the configured moderate count; and
6. a first pending coherent/non-static strong mask still applies D5 Oil safety without becoming public Foam, while static/incoherent/moderate-pending evidence does not; and
7. no raw score/mask diagnostic is lost.

## Gate 3 — retrospective composition

Prove:

1. Foam without numeric Oil remains `AUTHORITATIVE_FOAM` and unresolved;
2. two independently valid Foam-plus-Oil samples can establish confirmed-prior topology/direction;
3. the projected leading interval excludes the numeric Foam samples and preserves their observed Foam state;
4. hard unavailable/glare/failure flags still block even if malformed input also carries a numeric value;
5. opposite topology/direction remains conflict; and
6. no synthetic Oil number or detector-history mutation occurs.

## Gate 4 — report semantics

Verify `OIL_DROP_START` is labeled and described as the first **observed** sustained decrease. Its stored timestamp, event type, source capture and graph position must remain unchanged. Missing spans remain dashed display-only bridges and are not used to backdate the event.

Also prove that one already temporal-confirmed public Foam sample remains a bounded report episode, while an unflagged one-sample flicker remains omitted. Pending flags may connect confirmed observations and defer disappearance only inside the existing bounded dropout rule; they cannot start/backdate an episode, create a Foam coordinate or modify tracking/event history.

## Gate 5 — repository-local preservation

Run the complete Oil/Foam/retrospective/report suite and exact four-video replay. Reconcile every Foam and numeric Oil delta. Requirements:

- confirmed Oil truth remains at least `8/13` with no new gross error;
- sample2, true sample3 inflow Foam and sample4 Foam remain material episodes after bounded onset latency;
- Base and sample3 blind Foam-absent intervals remain Foam-free;
- sample3 full-state cap numerics remain absent;
- existing glare/collision/structure/no-interface negatives remain protected; and
- observation-density changes are not described as accuracy changes.

## Secure-Windows acceptance gate

Replay the same private Base/Accum bundle and record:

- exact, tolerant and reciprocal static overlap distributions;
- static-dominated, onset-pending, accepted Foam counts and episode timing;
- `FULL_WITH_FOAM`, `UNKNOWN_REVIEW`, numeric Oil and retrospective status distributions;
- first accepted Oil coordinates with source overlays and configured zero line; and
- observed Oil-drop timestamp versus the visual onset, explicitly retaining observation latency.

Expected direction, not a hard percentage target:

- Base residual fixed Foam publication should materially decline beyond R3's `32.1%` `FULL_WITH_FOAM` result;
- real Accum Foam should remain a bounded continuous episode rather than disappear;
- Foam-only false evidence should no longer block Oil routing or retrospective interpretation; and
- no claim about the reported coordinate offset is accepted without overlay agreement.

If registered overlap does not explain the remaining Base publication, stop and collect the earliest current-frame component metrics. Do not lower evidence or ambiguity thresholds.

## Acceptance boundary

Local gates may accept the implementation mechanism but cannot close S11. S11 remains `VALIDATING` until this exact secure-Windows replay and the final packaged field-workflow checklist pass.
