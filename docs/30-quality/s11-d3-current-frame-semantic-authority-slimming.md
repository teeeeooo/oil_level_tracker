## Implemented responsibility model

The accepted strong boundary route and the existing low-contrast recovery route remain sufficient paths. D3 is additive: failure of those routes no longer means that all weak represented evidence must be discarded.

Affirmative no-interface is resolved once before weak candidate comparison using the existing `0.58` acceptance floor and `0.10` dominance requirement. A subthreshold no-interface likelihood therefore cannot independently veto a visible Oil hypothesis. In the D3 comparative route, no-interface contributes only after qualification by normalized-raster uniformity.

The D3 weak route keeps direct observability/topology invalidity hard: candidate visibility and evidence availability must remain usable, severe glare/exclusion/border conflict is rejected, and accepted Foam-front topology remains authoritative. It then requires independent single-frame photometric corroboration: texture relief, phase-ceiling collision pressure and evidence reliability must establish that the raster contains distinguishable phase evidence.

For a hard-safe candidate, positive authority combines semantic boundary likelihood with independent corroboration once. Artifact likelihood and qualified absence are combined once as soft opposition. Broad strength, horizontal coverage, polarity, pulse/paired-edge morphology and ambiguity remain contributors to the existing semantic likelihood construction; they are not repeated as separate hard vetoes in the D3 route.

Comparative recovery is bounded to the existing semantic anchor neighborhood, at most one `maximum_proposal_diameter_px` from the leading semantic hypothesis. Independent texture therefore cannot relocate authority to a distant weak alternative. When locally neighboring candidates remain within the existing `0.08` authority band, D3 fails closed instead of choosing a tiny rank advantage.
