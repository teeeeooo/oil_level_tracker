## Feasibility review and adopted refinements

1. **Check representability before full integration.** The added input is
   operator-attributed structure support, a meaningful difference from the
   closed image-only proposals. However, all four original Mac recipes contain
   zero artifact templates, and the existing UI demo is `mixed_or_uncertain`.
   This bounded spot check establishes no eligible real `reviewed_support`.
   `OilMeasurementLineage` currently preserves measurements/band geometry, not
   general candidate-owned observed-edge masks. D2-A0 must map each actual
   candidate family to available evidence and identify the smallest reuse seam.
   Do not fabricate a current edge set from every Canny pixel inside its band.
   Missing prerequisites may still permit synthetic/input-contract work, but
   block a real efficacy comparison. Do not reopen the closed 0/28 s Foam or
   f1260 semicircle judgments to manufacture a positive control.
2. **Separate three outcomes.** D2-A completion means correct lineage, nulls,
   reproducibility, bounded transport and unchanged detector output. D2-B may
   show added information or diagnose that exact-pixel overlap is uninformative.
   Physical efficacy requires a separately declared observable/decision and
   abstention rule, then D3/O2 controls. The proposal's “all unresolved” no-go
   applies to that physical decision, not to a trace-only v1 whose decisions
   intentionally remain `NOT_EVALUATED`. Conversely successful raw statistics
   cannot satisfy O2 or justify a veto. Treat 1 px sensitivity as a feasibility
   risk; do not retune dilation/tolerance after seeing results.
3. **Define comparison domains and denominators.** Exact comparison is confined
   to reviewed reference scope and the common usable raster domain. Outside the
   reference scope is unreviewed, not candidate-only evidence of a new object.
   Effective/glare masks express sampling eligibility, not optical visibility
   through Foam or an overlapping fluid. Keep observed edges, native measurement
   footprints, processed support and centered envelopes distinct. Report
   availability per candidate family/basis, missing support and ambiguous
   overlap; never drop unavailable candidates from an apparent success rate.
4. **Freeze identities and budgets before integration.** Code confirms that
   snapshot hash alone omits review state/rectangle; the new consumer must bind
   both plus frame/candidate, geometry, preprocessing and comparator identity.
   A bound reference or temporal-registration fallback `(0,0)` is not verified
   correspondence. Current preflight correctly excludes behavior-neutral
   metadata; only a future behavioral consumer makes its reviewed digest/version
   part of runtime identity. The proposal supplies no measured incremental
   latency/RSS/trace budget: measure a fixed baseline and declare caps before
   integration. Existing capture storage limits are not that runtime budget.

These refinements do not require a new classifier, tracker, user workflow or ML.
Reuse the existing reference reader, candidate generation/lineage sidecars,
debug projector and W3 evaluator. If a family needs new extraction, explain why
the existing measurement owner cannot provide it; keep it debug-only and bounded.
