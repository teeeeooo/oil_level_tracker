## Scope

S11-D5 is a bounded S5-B current-frame authority-composition repair. It does not redesign S5-A Foam publication, `FoamTemporalGate`, S5-B temporal reduction, truth/Recipe schemas, dependencies, detector settings, or external result contracts.

The post-D4 effectiveness reconciliation exposed a discontinuity: without accepted Foam context, the accepted D2 low-contrast/Spatial and D3 comparative Oil authorities could recover hard-safe current-frame boundaries; once an authoritative Foam front and component arrived through D1, `evaluate_typed_current_observation()` selected `_select_foam_separated_boundary()` instead of those existing authorities. Spatial fallback passed the same context into that stricter branch. Therefore a valid Oil candidate could disappear solely because Foam context changed which semantic stack was consulted.

## D5 composition model

D5 removes that routing discontinuity without weakening Foam safety:

1. Existing S5-B hypotheses, semantic likelihoods, D2 scalar authority and D3 comparative authority remain the Oil semantic owners.
2. An authoritative Foam front is applied as an existing-style hard topology constraint: Oil at or above the Foam front cannot publish.
3. Accepted Foam-component pixels are removed from the mask used by independent single-frame Oil identifiability corroboration. This prevents Foam pixels from supplying texture/collision proof to Oil while leaving the semantic hypothesis representation unchanged.
4. Existing no-interface authority and visibility/glare/exclusion/border hard safety remain unchanged.
5. D3 local near-tie fail-closed behavior remains unchanged when candidates retain comparable authority. Authoritative Foam may separate a prior tie only when mandatory Foam-pixel exclusion changes the independent residual evidence.
6. The existing Foam-separated phase-support selector remains a final auxiliary route after D2/D3 are evaluated under Foam constraints; it no longer replaces D2/D3 merely because Foam exists.
7. Spatial fallback continues to remove Foam-component pixels from its x-resolved path proof. D5 therefore does not force a numeric Oil result when Foam consumes the independent sectors required by D2.

No new duplicated set of semantic thresholds was introduced.
