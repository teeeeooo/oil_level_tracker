- a saturated upper or lower Glass cap can no longer obtain canonical, comparative, anchor, no-interface-opposition or Spatial Oil authority; and

## Design basis

R3 was derived from the accepted S11 responsibility model, direct MP4/Recipe/truth review and the prior external review recorded in the [implementation reference log](../../70-reference/implementation-reference-log.md). The external work supports relative phase evidence, multi-line/path consistency, controlled illumination and explicit uncertainty, but does not justify treating every coherent horizontal path as a fluid interface. R3 therefore adds opposition at the existing structural and Foam publication seams rather than introducing a new model or broadly relaxing thresholds.

## Implemented responsibilities

### Dark Glass-cap invalidity

The current-frame Oil owner now identifies the bounded conjunction of border proximity, saturated dark border phase and materially brighter interior. Such hypotheses remain visible in diagnostic observations but are removed before canonical selection, D2/D3 comparison, anchor/neighborhood selection, no-interface competition and Spatial fallback. A separate lower-phase support floor applies only to weak D3 lower-rim recovery outside authoritative Foam context.

Two-sided controls prove that ordinary interior transitions, non-saturated border phases and the retained near-bottom sample2 interface remain eligible. This is a topology/photometry invalidity class, not a preferred Oil polarity or a global border penalty.

### Learned static-Foam opposition

`OpenCvPhaseDetector` now learns a per-Glass `uint8` static-Foam map from the same bounded start/middle/end preparation frames already used for static artifact learning. Only candidate-bearing strong or moderate S5-A support present in all three representative frames enters the map; weak, ambiguous and glare-rejected masks remain empty. A later accepted component with at least `0.80` support overlap receives `STATIC_REJECTED`: its score, mask and overlap remain diagnostic, while public Foam, tracker update, fill-state authority and S5-B Foam context are withheld. Reset-one/reset-all preserve per-Glass lifecycle isolation.

The checked-in four-video corpus did not require this static opposition to obtain its final deltas. Its local evidence is therefore controlled-fixture evidence only; effectiveness on the nearly invariant private Base Glass remains a secure-Windows residual.

### Row-coherent Foam publication

A raw accepted Foam component must now prove layer-like row topology. It publishes when materially wide support exists on at least half of its rows, or when a narrower component is compact enough to preserve genuine partial Foam. Fragmented broad bounding boxes receive `INCOHERENT_REJECTED`, retaining raw score/mask diagnostics without downstream state authority.

This removes the late sample3 warm/refractive components while retaining the true sample3 inflow/agitation layer, the compact sample2 layer and the accepted sample4 Foam surface.
